AI Slop Detector, v0.2.0
Lightly blurs images and videos that look AI-generated while you scroll, so you notice them.
Click the "Likely AI · N% · show" chip to see one clearly; hover the chip to see why it was flagged.
Everything runs on your computer. The only download is the detection model, once, on first run.

INSTALL (Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer)
1. Move this "ai-slop-detector" folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents.
   Chrome runs it from this folder, so deleting the folder removes the extension.
2. In Chrome, open:  chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (switch at the top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this "ai-slop-detector" folder (the one with manifest.json in it).
5. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to AI Slop Detector.
6. Click its icon once. The first time, it downloads its detection model (about 44 MB).
   The menu shows "downloading model", then "ready".

SETTINGS (click the icon)
- Blur likely AI: on or off.
- Blur strength: Light (default), Medium, Strong. The more confident it is, the blurrier.
- Sensitivity: Sensitive (flags at 50%), Balanced (65%, default), Strict (80%).
- Badges: Flagged only (default) or Every image.
- Check videos: on (default) or off.
- "Disable on this site" turns it off for the site you're on.

GOOD TO KNOW
- It's a strong guess, not proof. Some AI images will get through and some real ones will get flagged.
- It skips small things like profile pictures and emoji (anything shown under 100 pixels).
- Videos are checked every 1.5 seconds while they're on screen, and flagged on the average of a few frames.
- Text posts aren't checked.
- Chrome may sometimes warn about "developer mode extensions" when it starts. That's normal for
  extensions installed this way; keep it.

Based on Detectra by ashhart (https://github.com/ashhart/Detectra), MIT license, see LICENSE.
Changes: confidence-scaled light blur, chip instead of duplicate badges, badges on flagged items only,
