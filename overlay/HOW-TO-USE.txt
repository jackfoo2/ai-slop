AI Slop Overlay, v0.15.1
Lightly blurs pictures and videos that look AI-generated, anywhere on your screen.

NEEDS: Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.

START IT
1. Close the old version first: X in the top-left corner.
2. Put this "AI Slop Overlay" folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents (delete the old folder).
3. Double-click "AI Slop Overlay.exe" once. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click
   "More info", then "Run anyway". The desktop icon then points at this copy; use it from now on.

CONTROLS
- Top-left corner: "AI Slop  On  X". On / Off turns the blurring on and off. X stops the program completely.
- Opening it from the desktop icon while it's running just switches it back on.
- Ctrl+Alt+B also turns it on and off.
- Right-click the tray icon (bottom-right of the taskbar) for blur strength, sensitivity, videos,
  "Turn off during games", the log folder, and Quit.
- Every blurred picture has an X in its top-right corner: click it to remove that blur.
  (Clicking "Likely AI · N% · show" does the same.)
- It also reads text in and around each picture: AI watermarks ("Imagined with AI", "KLING AI", "Sora",
  "Veo"...) and site labels or hashtags ("AI info", "AI-generated", #aiart, #midjourney...). A hit blurs the
  picture no matter what the detector thought, and the tag says why ("Watermark · kling ai"). It reads one
  picture at a time while it sits still, so give it a few seconds. Tray > "Read labels and watermarks" to switch off.
- It recognizes your own desktop wallpaper and leaves it alone, even when windows cover part of it.
  (The same picture opened somewhere else is still checked.) Tray > "Ignore my desktop wallpaper" to change that.
- Tray > "CPU limit": 10%, 20% (default), 35% or no limit. It measures its own CPU use (the number Task Manager
  shows) and checks less often when it's over. It also does almost nothing while the screen is still.
- RATE IT: every tag has + and -. + means it got that picture right, - means it got it wrong.
  - on a blurred picture unblurs it (you're saying it's real). With "Show scores on every picture" on, the grey
  tags have + and - too: - there means it missed an AI picture, and it blurs it. Your call sticks to that picture,
  even after a restart. Each rating saves a small copy of the picture and its scores on your PC only
  (tray > "Open ratings folder"); the tray shows how often it was right.
- TELL IT SOMETHING: the … button on any tag opens a box for one sentence, like "this is AI", "this is a real
  photo", "anything like this is AI", "stop blurring in Discord", "too many false alarms", "blur more" or
  "stop blurring profile pics". It does it and shows what it did, with Undo. Every sentence is saved with the picture
  (ratings folder, feedback.jsonl) so it can be reviewed later. Common wordings are understood instantly; anything
  else goes to a small AI language model that runs on your PC. The first time that happens it downloads the model
  once (about 480 MB, from the official Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct repo on Hugging Face) and then works offline.
  Its answer is limited to the same short list of actions; if it can't tell, your sentence is kept as a note.
- Tray > "Check picture files…": pick pictures saved on your PC and see the detector's score for each, at full
  size and shrunk to feed size. Useful for finding out why something wasn't blurred.
- Tray > "Show scores on every picture" puts a small grey score on every picture it finds, blurred or not.
  Handy when it misses one: no score means it didn't find the picture; a low score means the detector
  didn't think it was AI. Tray > Sensitivity > Sensitive blurs from 50% instead of 65%.

GAMES
While a game is running, the overlay gets out of the way completely: it stops capturing the screen and
removes its window, so nothing sits on top of the game. Only the tray icon stays. It comes back on its own
10 seconds after the game closes. It counts as a game when:
- an anti-cheat program is running (Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye, Riot Vanguard, FACEIT, EA Anti-Cheat,
  GameGuard, PunkBuster, Tencent ACE), even if you alt-tab out of the game,
- the app in front is installed from Steam, Epic, Riot, EA, Ubisoft, GOG, Xbox, Rockstar or Roblox,
- or something other than a browser or video player is running full screen.
The tray icon shows why it's off. If Riot Vanguard is installed and always running, it will stay off.
For the strictest competitive games, the only 100% guarantee is quitting it (X) before you play.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Screenshots won't show the blur, on purpose. Take a phone photo of the screen to show what you see.
- Main monitor only for now.

IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
Tray icon > "Open log folder" > send overlay-log.txt.

Detection model: Detectra v3 by ashhart (MIT). Built with Electron and ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).
